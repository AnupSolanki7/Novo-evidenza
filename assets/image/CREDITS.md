## Selection notes

Candidates were rejected where they would have misrepresented Vivoclin:

- A clinician photo was discarded because the lab coat read "Urologic Oncology
  Branch — National Cancer Institute", which would imply an affiliation that
  does not exist.
- A laboratory photo was discarded for prominent third-party vendor branding on
  the consumables.

These are illustrative stock photographs of clinical and laboratory settings.
They are not photographs of Vivoclin facilities, staff, or studies, and should
not be captioned as such.
